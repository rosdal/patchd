# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities in Patchd itself privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/rosdal/patchd/security/advisories/new). Don't open a public issue.

We acknowledge reports within 2 business days and aim to fix confirmed high and critical issues within 30 days. We credit reporters in the advisory unless asked not to.

## Safe harbour

We won't pursue legal action against research done in good faith that avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, and that gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release receives security fixes.
