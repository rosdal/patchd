# Code of conduct

Patchd adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to follow it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines when deciding on consequences.
